# What we heard: community session 1

> Plain-language summary sent to participants before anything is shared publicly. Fictional session.

Thank you for taking part. Here is what we heard. Please tell us if we got anything wrong.

## About the part that remembers your settings

1. Setting up things like text size and captions again on every site and every device is tiring.
2. You want to see everything it remembers, in plain words, and delete things.
3. It should remember the tools you use. It should never record or suggest a reason why you use them.
4. Before you agree, you want a simple explanation of where your settings are kept. You also want to be able to take them with you.

## About the changes it makes to websites

5. With bigger text, the website's menu still gets hidden or pushed off the screen.
6. When it changes something, like turning a chart into a table, it should tell you.
7. The new table needs a name, so you can get to it using voice control.
8. People want different things: some want to be asked first every time, some want to be asked once, and some want changes to happen automatically with a short notice.

## About when an AI assistant should stop and check with you

9. It should always stop before anything involving money, and explain the difference in a short, simple way.
10. You need an easy way to undo a change. A big, obvious one.

## Three questions

- **Did we get this right?**
- **Is there anything here you would not want shared publicly?** Anything you approve may appear, without your name, in tasks on a public website where developers work on the toolkit.
- **Did we miss anything?**

You can reply however suits you: by email, a voice message, or a short call. Please reply by [date].
